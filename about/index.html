About Froots

Grown in Munich.

All began with two seeds in the ground. One had a lot of skank — the other had deep pockets. And as they kept growing, both ideas intertwined, grew together and didn't let go. The froots from it are magnificent — in various colors, shapes and sizes. But if you put one to your ear and listen closely, you'll hear a pulse. A musical pulse.

Recommended: Put them in a basket, amplify, and enjoy.

Our little musical ecosystem.

Our Reggae influence comes from Tribal Seeds and Stick Figure.

Our Funk is unmistakably drawn from the Vulfiverse — Vulfpeck, Vulfmon, The Fearless Flyers — and legendary '70s Motown Funk&Soul.

While we enjoy riding the legendary trains of our heros, we're aimed at writing our own music. This includes (but is not limited to) our signature mix of both Reggae and Funk.

Our member requirement: gardening skills and pants with deep pockets.
